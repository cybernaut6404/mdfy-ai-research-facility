# `data/` — judge reports + derived analysis + figures

This directory contains the data that supports the manuscript's claims plus
the analysis artifacts derived by `make replicate`.

## Vendored (at unit-intake, 2026-05-24)

### `results/`

Per-channel judge reports (`results/<channel>/results.json` +
`results/<channel>/report.md`) from the 9 originally-validated channels'
substrate-paper baseline runs. Used by `../code/verify_kappa.py` to
recover κ values for the MATCH-tolerance check.

**Important scope limitation:** `results/` covers only the 9 originally-validated
channels at their single-layer baseline configuration. It does NOT cover:
- The 4 KILL-channels' multi-layer-rescue results (the manuscript's
  §3.3 "rescue" κ values)
- The 15 new channels' validation results (Dark Tetrad, HEXACO, attachment,
  locus, self-construct)

Those results live on the local SHA f492844 on the MacMini that was never
pushed to GitHub. See `../code/README.md` §"What's NOT vendored" and
`../replication-log.md` §"Honest gap surfaced by the local replicate"
for the closure path.

### `runs/` — NOT vendored

The raw generation pairs that feed `analyse.py` are NOT vendored. They are
regenerable via `make replicate-full` (which calls `code/experiments/d4-fader-intervention/harness.py`
on Modal L4). Estimated size if vendored: ~41MB across 100 subdirectories.
See `../code/README.md` §"What's NOT vendored" for the rationale.

## Derived (regenerated by `make replicate`)

### `derived/stats.{json,md}`

Per-channel statistical-significance analysis:
- Two-sided binomial test against chance (p = 0.50) on non-tie pairs.
- 95% Wilson score confidence interval on the high-win proportion.
- Holm-Bonferroni and Benjamini-Hochberg (BH-FDR) correction across the
  18-channel family with non-blank Table-S1 W/L/T counts.

**Headline:** 0 of 18 channels reach significance under Holm-Bonferroni or
BH-FDR at α = 0.05. Per-channel n is too small (2–22 non-tie pairs, median 7)
to clear the family-wise correction threshold. See `../code/compute_stats.py`
for the script and the manuscript §3.1.1 for the narrative implication.

### `derived/replication-delta.md`

Per-channel comparison of recovered κ (from vendored `results/*/results.json`)
vs published κ (from the manuscript's Table S1). Status one of:
- `MATCH` — within ±0.05 tolerance.
- `OUT_OF_TOLERANCE` — vendored results.json κ differs by > ±0.05 from the
  manuscript's published value. For the 4 KILL-rescued channels this is
  EXPECTED — the vendored data is single-layer baseline; the manuscript
  reports ML-rescued values from the unpushed local SHA.
- `NOT_VENDORED` — judge reports not in this bundle (the 15 new channels;
  also on the unpushed local SHA).

**Headline:** 5 MATCH · 4 OUT_OF_TOLERANCE (the 4 KILL-rescue channels) · 15
NOT_VENDORED (the new channels). See the file itself for the closure path
for the 4 OOT and 15 NOT_VENDORED rows.

### `figures/`

Three PNG figures regenerated from the manuscript's Table S1/S2/S3 data:

- `fig1_kappa_forest.png` — κ forest plot for all 24 channels with 95%
  Wilson CIs, coloured by category, with κ = 0.50 chance line and
  κ = 0.60 PASS gate. The visual width of the CIs makes the small-N
  constraint immediate (even the κ = 1.000 rows have lower-bound CIs at
  ≈ 0.44).
- `fig2_cosine_heatmap_L16.png` — inter-channel cosine heatmap for the
  curated subset reported in Table S3 (the H × Dark-Triad coupling subset).
  Full 15×15 or 24×24 matrix expansion is named as a Tier-2-readiness
  follow-up in manuscript §2.7.
- `fig3_refusal_cosine.png` — per-channel worst-layer
  |cos(channel, refusal)| from Table S2, sorted, coloured by SAFE/watch/FLAG
  status, with the |cos| = 0.30 FLAG threshold drawn.

Embedded in MANUSCRIPT.md at §3.1, §3.7, §3.6 respectively.

## Sensitivity of the data artifacts

- **The contrastive items in `../code/infra/steering-vectors/contrastive-items/`**
  encode the author's single-subject operationalisation of each trait
  (HEXACO, Dark Tetrad, attachment, etc.). Release implies disclosure of
  that operationalisation; the author has elected this per the n=1 framing
  of the manuscript (see manuscript §2.2 and §8.1). No third-party PII is
  present.
- **The probe libraries in `../code/experiments/d4-fader-intervention/probes/`**
  include scenario prompts designed to elicit dark traits (machiavellianism,
  narcissism, psychopathy, sadism). Per manuscript §4.6, these are
  research-evaluation materials, not deployment-ready content. Release is
  consistent with the unit's content license (CC-BY-4.0) and the manuscript's
  Broader Impacts mitigations.
- **The 9-channel `results/*/judgements.jsonl` files** contain per-pair raw
  judge responses (A / B / TIE) plus the response text for each pair. The
  response text is Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct's output under steering; no
  third-party content.
